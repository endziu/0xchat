# Install the app

You can add 0xChat to your home screen or desktop so it opens in its own window. You can also
keep using it in your browser without installing it. On iPhone and iPad, you need to install
it to receive notifications.

## Android, Chrome and Edge

Select **Install** in the banner near the top of 0xChat, or open your browser's menu and choose
**Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open 0xChat in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.

## Keep your existing address

The installed app may save its data separately from your browser. Before switching,
[back up your key](./your-key.md#back-up-your-key). If the installed app shows a different
address, [import your saved key](./your-key.md#import-a-key) to use your existing account.
