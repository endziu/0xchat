# Install the app

Installing gives hushhash its own window and home screen icon. It's optional, except on iPhone and
iPad, where you need it for notifications.

## Android, Chrome and Edge

Select **Install** in the banner above your chats, or choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home
screen**) from your browser's menu.

## iPhone and iPad

In Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

## Keep your address

The installed app may not share storage with your browser.
[Back up your key](./your-key.md#back-up-your-key) first, and if the installed app shows a
different address, [import it](./your-key.md#import-a-key).
