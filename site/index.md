# What is 0xChat?

0xChat is a private messenger for conversations that don't need to last. Messages are end-to-end
encrypted and delete themselves on a timer you choose. There is no sign-up: no email, phone
number or password.

## How it works

- **Your address is your contact.** The app creates one the first time you open it. It starts
  with `0x`, and anyone who has it can message you.
- **Only you and the other person can read your messages.** They are encrypted in your browser
  before they are sent. The server delivers them but cannot read them.
- **Messages expire.** You choose how long each message lasts once it's opened, from 5 seconds
  to 24 hours. Unopened messages are deleted after 24 hours.
- **Your key is your account.** It's stored in your browser. Back it up to keep your address,
  or skip the backup and treat the account as disposable.
